How I Approach Automation

Understand the operation. Then automate it.

Technology can accelerate a business. First, you have to understand what it is accelerating.

I believe effective automation begins with a deep understanding of how a business actually operates—not just its individual processes, but how people, systems, information, resources, and decisions interact across the entire organization. Before deploying technology or AI-driven solutions, it's essential to understand the dependencies, bottlenecks, exceptions, and inefficiencies that influence operational performance.

Automating a poorly understood process doesn't fix its problems; it simply allows those problems to happen faster and at greater scale. My approach is to first understand the complete operational ecosystem, identify opportunities for meaningful improvement, and then design solutions that integrate naturally into the business.

True scalability comes from building the right operational foundation before applying automation—not the other way around.
